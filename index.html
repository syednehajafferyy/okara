What a TMR mixer does

TMR stands for total mixed ration. Rather than feeding forage, grain and supplements separately, the mixer combines them so every mouthful is the same.

  1. Fill the mixer with forage, grain and supplements.
  2. The hydraulic drive turns the augers until the batch is even.
  3. Discharge the finished ration along the feed lane.
